\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
	\item We propose a fusion strategy combining the Mel spectrogram and Gramian Angular Difference Field of transformer voiceprint signals. Gamma correction is applied to enhance the Mel features of the fused image, and a Pulse Coupled Neural Network is employed to improve feature discriminability while suppressing noise interference.
	\item We design an improved AlexNet-SE architecture within the PINNs framework to achieve accurate fault diagnosis. Specifically, batch normalization and Squeeze-and-Excitation channel attention modules are integrated after each convolutional layer to refine structural representation and enhance channel feature extraction.
	\item Extensive experiments validate the effectiveness of the proposed method, achieving high recognition accuracy and demonstrating robustness compared with conventional approaches.
\end{itemize}
