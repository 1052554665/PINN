		\opening{Dear Editor,}
	
	On behalf of all co-authors, I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textit{``Acoustic Condition Monitoring and Fault Diagnosis of Power Transformers via Multi-Modal Fusion and Physics-Informed Representation Learning''} for consideration for publication in the \textit{Journal of Power Electronics}.
	
	Acoustic-based transformer diagnosis faces three challenges: insufficient feature extraction, background noise, and limited labeled data. We address them through (i) three-channel Mel--GADF fusion with gamma correction and a pulse-coupled neural network (PCNN) front-end for noise suppression; (ii) an improved AlexNet-SE backbone regularized by a physics-informed Laplacian term derived from the acoustic Helmholtz equation; and (iii) rigorous validation, including component-wise ablation, session-aware five-fold cross-validation, and comparisons with several state-of-the-art models. On a real-world 220~kV transformer dataset, the method achieves a recognition accuracy of 98.48\%.
	
	The manuscript falls within the journal's scope of power quality and utility applications, analysis, modeling and control, and other applications, extending recent JPE work on signal-processing-based diagnostics of power equipment.
	
	We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission, and there are no conflicts of interest to declare.
	
	We appreciate your consideration and look forward to your response.
